      Changes records of both spaces as one: those keys names, and those watch names (in either space) from what they hold. change answers the next of each, by space in the order read; it runs again on a race, so it is pure, and a record it leaves out is kept. Every record read is held to what was read; a record named twice is read once and written from one naming at most.
