    bare

    • bare(request: HttpRequest): boolean

      Whether the request carries nothing a client authenticates with, whichever methods are composed: no authorization header, no client certificate (malformed included), none of the credential parameters (client_id, client_secret, client_assertion, client_assertion_type) sent at all, and nothing a composed method claims or finds malformed. no_credentials from authenticate says only that no composed method claimed it.
