    accessTokenLifetimeSeconds

    • accessTokenLifetimeSeconds(): number

      The access-token lifetime that issue applies where the grant gives no lifetimeSeconds, in whole seconds from 2 to 86400. It is fixed when tokens is built and never changes after, so a check made at build time stays true. It is not the longest life any token may have: a grant's own lifetimeSeconds and token exchange's cap may differ. A boot check against it does not reach tokens already issued, or instances still on other settings.

    issue

    • issue(request: IssuanceRequest, limits?: IssueLimits): Promise<Issued>

      Issues once every policy, its own first, allows it; throws Unavailable when one cannot tell. The token's iat is when issue was asked, before the policies are, and its lifetime runs from there, their wait included; what it answers is left runs from signing. Refuses, as its own rule, a token with less than a second left once signed, its cap passed, its lifetime spent in the policies' wait or its signing slow: policy tokens, reason no_lifetime_left, the client's word expired. Such a token is handed to no one, and nothing of it is kept.
