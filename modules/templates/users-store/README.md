# users-store

Wires `users`' store interfaces to `subject-records`' records, kept without end, over the phase this process reads once: links in `users-store.links` (`online-indexed`, each listed in its subject's index, many a subject's) and verified contacts in `users-store.contacts` (`online-keyed`, keyed by the subject itself), both erased by `users` and declared beside their index spaces; and the link store's `issuers` (IdP names' own records, in `users-store.names`, `records`' records declared as holding no subject's data) and `unowned` (a name's record kept raw in the link space until its records are indexed, then `closed`; deprecated from birth). The link store offers its `remove`, `erase`, `migrate`, `issuers` and `unowned` as whole sets, and the contact store its `erase` and `migrate`, each straight from the builder. Each space declares a guarantee for each need `users` states for it. A need with nothing mapped to it fails the type check, and `users`' store contracts, run on a store that loses what a missing guarantee allows, in both phases, check what each guarantee means. The composition is out of traffic while the phase cannot be read (probe `users-store.phase`). The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. Depends on `kernel`, `users`, `records`, `subject-records` and `store`.
