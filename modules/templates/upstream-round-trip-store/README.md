# upstream-round-trip-store

Wires `upstream-round-trip`'s store interface, round trips, to `records`' records under a secret, so a round trip's cookie never reaches the store. Its one space declares a guarantee for each need `upstream-round-trip` states, and the store's `walk` for erasure is records' own. The space holds no subject's data (`none`): a round trip a 0.19 instance began may, until its record's lifetime ends, and upstream-round-trip's migration stops until none remains. A need with nothing mapped to it, a guarantee or records under a secret, fails the type check, and `upstream-round-trip`'s store contract, run on a store that loses what a missing guarantee allows, checks what each guarantee means. The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. Depends on `kernel`, `upstream-round-trip`, `records` and `store`.
