# mfa-flows-store

Wires `mfa-flows`' store interfaces to `subject-records`: transactions and release permits to records under a secret, so an identifier never reaches the store (`bounded` by `storedLifetimeMaxMs`, the longer of a transaction's 30 min and a permit's hour, and erased by `mfa-flows`: a record is fenced at its save by its subject's tombstone, and one saved before the erasure ends by its own end), and each holder's transaction listings (`online-indexed`, erased by `mfa-flows`) to keyed sets of up to the section's `transactions-per-holder` (1–10000, default 5; design.md's rule that wiring capacities are settings with defaults) on `kernel`'s clock, whose members are digests, so no secret names them. Each has its own space, declaring a guarantee for each of the other needs `mfa-flows` states; the listings' declares confirmed reads, so the store answers them only from writes a failover keeps. A need with nothing mapped to it fails the type check, and `mfa-flows`' store contracts, run on a store that loses what a missing guarantee allows, check what each guarantee means. Both spaces declare `subject-records`' `tombstoneFloor` beside their needs, as their index spaces hold the subject's tombstone, and are read over the phase this process reads once; the composition is out of traffic until it reads (probe `mfa-flows-store.phase`), and every port, the erase and migrate ports too, comes straight from the builders. The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. Depends on `kernel`, `mfa-flows`, `subject-records` and `store`.
