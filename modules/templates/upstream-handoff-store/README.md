# upstream-handoff-store

Wires `upstream-handoff`'s store interface, held logins, to `subject-records`' records under a secret, over the phase this process reads once, so a held login's carry never reaches the store. Its one space, `upstream-handoff-store.held-logins`, is declared `bounded` by `heldLoginLifetimeMaxMs` and erased by `upstream-handoff`, beside its index space: a held login is fenced at its save by its subject's tombstone, and one saved before the erasure ends by its own end. Every port comes straight from the builder. The space declares a guarantee for each need `upstream-handoff` states, and `subject-records`' `tombstoneFloor` beside them, as its index space holds the subject's tombstone with the same guarantees. A need with nothing mapped to it, a guarantee or records under a secret, fails the type check, and `upstream-handoff`'s store contract, run on a store that loses what a missing guarantee allows, in both phases, checks what each guarantee means. The composition is out of traffic while the phase cannot be read (probe `upstream-handoff-store.phase`). The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. Depends on `kernel`, `upstream-handoff`, `subject-records` and `store`.
