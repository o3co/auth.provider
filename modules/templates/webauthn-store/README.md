# webauthn-store

Wires `passkeys`' store interfaces to `subject-records`' and `records`' records, over the phase this process reads once. Passkeys are kept without end in two spaces, `webauthn-store.credentials` and `webauthn-store.subjects`, each declared `online-indexed` and erased by `passkeys`, beside its index space; records of both are changed as one through `subject-records`' joint change (`updateAsOne`; a store that cannot write records of two partitions as one refuses the boot, as `subject-records` tells it). Registration challenges are kept in `webauthn-store.registration-challenges`, declared `bounded` at the time `passkeys` states it keeps one (`challengeStoreNeeds.registrationsEndWithinMs`) and erased by `passkeys`, under a secret. Assertion challenges stay records under a secret in `webauthn-store.challenges`, declared `none`: who asked, a digest of its id and a time; a node before 0.20 also keeps a subject's registration challenges there, each ending within that time of its opening; waiting them out before erasure is enabled is account erasure's to check, not this module's. Each space declares a guarantee for each need `passkeys` states, and the indexed and bounded ones `subject-records`' `tombstoneFloor` beside them. Every port of the passkey and registration challenge stores comes straight from the builders, `erase` and `migrate` among them. The composition is out of traffic while the phase cannot be read (probe `webauthn-store.phase`). A need stated `true` with nothing mapped to it fails the type check (a need of another value, such as a bound, is no guarantee), and `passkeys`' store contracts, run on a store that loses what a missing guarantee allows, in both phases, check what each guarantee means. The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. The passkey and assertion spaces keep the names webauthn's records had, so what was kept reads as it is. Depends on `kernel`, `passkeys`, `subject-records`, `records` and `store`.
